About

I work where people, process, data, and systems meet.

I am a systems and operations professional who finds meaning in making complicated work easier for people to understand, manage, and improve.

I am especially drawn to ambiguity: when information lives in several places, teams hold different pieces of the process, and the real structure has not yet been defined. I bring those perspectives together, uncover the system beneath the confusion, and turn it into something people can use.

My work spans process architecture, ERP strategy, production planning, quality-data systems, SQL reporting, operational dashboards, and cross-functional implementation. Across each of these areas, my approach is the same: people are at the center of the system, and the system should help them do their work well.
